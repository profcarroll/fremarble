BUG-002: accelerometer axes transposed. A left/right tilt moved the marble up/down.

Evidence:
1. The N900 accelerometer table (wiki.maemo.org/N900_accelerometer, sysfs coord, mg):
     lying on its back            0     0  -1000
     right edge down          -1000     0      0
     left edge down            1000     0      0
     bottom edge down             0 -1000      0
   so device x runs along the LONG side of the screen (left/right) and device y along the
   SHORT side (top/bottom). The device on its charger reads (18, -468, -756): leaning back
   32 degrees about its long axis, bottom edge down, on y. Consistent with the table.
2. game.py, tools/marble_fps.py and docs/TASK-tune.md all had
     vx += -y_mg ; vy += -x_mg
   i.e. screen x driven by the short-side axis and screen y by the long-side axis. The
   comment in marble_fps.py said "sign found by trying"; the signs were right, the pairing
   was not, and nobody with the device in hand had checked direction against intent.
3. telemetry/human2.csv reads coherently once the axes are swapped back. The player's
   first sustained tilt (t=3.3..5.8 s) was x=-270, y=+186 after calibration: RIGHT along the
   corridor, toward the goal, with a little "up". The marble went to the bottom-LEFT corner
   (58,142) and stayed there. At t=5.9 the player pitched the device hard forward
   (y=-1000): the marble ran right. At t=7.5 they pitched it back (y=+972): the marble ran
   left and hit the wall it had left at 473 px/s. They quit at 9 s.
4. telemetry/human1.csv (120 s, old physics): where the player needed to go DOWN the shaft,
   their tilt agreed with the old y mapping only 26% of the time.

Fix (game.py, tools/marble_fps.py):
   screen_ax = -x_mg ; screen_ay = -y_mg     (marble rolls toward the low edge)
   vx += screen_ax * ACCEL_PER_MG * dt ; vy += screen_ay * ACCEL_PER_MG * dt
Also: the dead zone is now soft (|tilt| < 40 mg -> 0, otherwise 40 mg subtracted) so the
response is continuous at the edge of the zone instead of stepping from 0 to 48 px/s^2.
The bot script in README.md has x and y swapped to match. tools/tilt_axes.py prints the
direction the marble would roll from the live sensor so the next hand can confirm it in
ten seconds before playing.

Verification: python2.5 tools/tilt_axes.py on the device with the device tilted; and the
bot script "0,0,-1000:3;-300,0,-800:3;150,-300,-800:3;-300,-150,-800:3;150,100,-800:1.5;0,0,-1000:3"
(the old script with x and y swapped, plus a level lead-in for the calibration window)
reaching the goal of level 001 in 11.7 s, no deaths: telemetry/run6.csv.
